Got questions?

Frequently asked
questions.

What is eBudget?
eBudget is an Android app that helps you track grocery spending month by month. Log items, set a budget, record transport, and get monthly analytics — all synced to the cloud.
How do I install it? Is it on the Play Store?
eBudget is distributed as an APK — no Play Store required. After subscribing, you'll receive the APK. Enable "Install unknown apps" in Android settings, tap the file, and it installs like any other app.
What happens to my data if my subscription expires?
Your data is preserved in the cloud. If your subscription lapses, your account is suspended but all history is saved. Renew and everything is restored immediately.
How long does the promo price last?
The K14.99 launch promo applies for your first 3 months. After that it moves to the standard K29.99/month. Only available to the first 100 users.
How quickly is my account activated?
Immediately once your payment is confirmed. Send payment, then WhatsApp your name and number. As soon as payment is verified, your account is activated and the APK is sent.
Can I use it on multiple phones?
Yes. Since eBudget syncs to the cloud, sign in on any Android device and your full data is there. Install the APK on as many devices as you like — your subscription covers your account, not a single phone.
How do I renew?
Same process: send payment to +260 762 164 413 via MTN MoMo, then WhatsApp your name and number. Renewed immediately once payment is confirmed.
